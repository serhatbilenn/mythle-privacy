Mythle Support

Need help with Mythle, your mythology guessing and collection game? Contact us with questions, purchase issues, or bug reports.

Contact support

Email onursendur@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Mythle version, and a short description of the problem. Screenshots can help. Never send passwords or payment card details.

Premium purchases

If a previous Premium purchase is missing, open Profile → Settings → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. If you still need help, email us.

App will not open?

Try closing and reopening Mythle, then restarting your device. If the problem continues, contact us with the details above. Avoid deleting the app: your collection and progress are stored locally.
